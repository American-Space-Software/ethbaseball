# Seasons, Rewards, and Player Development

---

EBL is built around a simple loop:

Build your team.

Play games.

Earn rewards.

Improve your players.

Repeat.

Here's how that works. ⚾🧵

---

Each season lasts 162 days.

Every day at 9:30 AM Eastern Time, the league advances to a new day.

---

Before games can be played, your team must enter the queue.

The queue is where teams wait to be matched against opponents.

---

When a matchup is found, the game begins immediately.

Teams can play up to one game per league day.

---

EBL uses team ratings to find opponents.

Teams with similar ratings are generally matched together.

---

Once a matchup is created, the game is simulated automatically.

Managers don't need to be online while games are played.

---

Every game produces a winner and a loser.

Those results affect team ratings and league standings.

---

Games also produce rewards.

Teams that participate in competitive matchups earn Diamonds.

---

A close matchup currently earns approximately 100 Diamonds per team.

Both teams receive rewards regardless of who wins the game.

---

There are also season-end rewards.

On the final day of the season, qualifying teams receive 50 Diamonds for every win they earned during the year.

---

To qualify for season-end rewards, a team must complete at least 130 games during the season.

Teams that fall below that threshold are not eligible.

---

Diamonds are the primary currency used throughout EBL.

Most long-term franchise decisions involve deciding how to use them.

---

One option is player development.

Every franchise has a player development setting.

---

That setting determines what percentage of your daily Diamond rewards are automatically directed toward player development.

You decide how aggressive that setting should be.

---

Higher development spending helps players improve faster.

Lower development spending leaves more Diamonds available for other uses.

---

Players also gain experience by playing.

As players participate in games, they earn XP and progress toward their next level.

---

Each player has an XP bar that tracks that progress.

When the bar fills, the player gains a level and their ratings improve.

---

Development spending and experience work together.

Both contribute to long-term player growth.

---

Over time, those improvements can strengthen your roster.

The strongest franchises are usually built over many seasons, not a single day.

---

Improved players can help your team win more games.

Winning more games can lead to more rewards.

---

More rewards create more opportunities to improve your franchise.

---

That creates a continuous cycle.

Games generate rewards.

Rewards support development.

Development strengthens the roster.

A stronger roster can help win more games.
