# Marketplace

---

The marketplace is where managers buy and sell players using Diamonds.

Here's how it works. ⚾🧵

---

No roster stays the same forever.

The marketplace allows managers to acquire players from other franchises and reshape their teams over time.

---

Managers can create sales listings for players they are willing to sell.

Other managers can then purchase those players.

---

Listings are visible to everyone.

Any manager can browse the available players and review their ratings, age, and development progress.

---

Managers can also send offers directly to other franchises.

An offer allows you to target a specific player even if that player is not listed for sale.

---

An offer specifies a player and a Diamond amount.

The receiving manager can decide whether to accept or reject it.

---

Many managers also use the Discord server to discuss potential deals.

The #marketplace channel is a common place to advertise players, look for buyers, and find trade partners.

---

This gives managers multiple ways to acquire players.

Purchase a listed player, send an offer, or connect with other managers through Discord.

---

Every acquisition has a cost.

Spending Diamonds on one player means those Diamonds are no longer available elsewhere.

---

Because of that, managers constantly evaluate tradeoffs.

Is a player worth the asking price?

---

Age matters.

Ratings matter.

Future development matters.

---

A player who helps today may not be the same player who helps most three seasons from now.

---

There is no single correct approach.

Every franchise decides how it wants to build its roster.

---

The marketplace creates movement throughout the league.

Players regularly change teams as managers pursue different strategies.

---

Some managers focus on improving immediately.

Others focus on acquiring younger players and developing them over time.

---

Successful teams earn Diamonds through gameplay.

Those Diamonds can then be used to acquire players.

---

Not every successful franchise spends its Diamonds the same way.

Some focus on player development.

Others focus on player acquisition.

---

The marketplace gives managers another way to shape the future of their franchise.

---

Every franchise begins with a roster.

What happens to that roster is up to the manager.
