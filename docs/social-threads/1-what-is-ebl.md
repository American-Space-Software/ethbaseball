# What is EBL?

---

EBL was built on a simple idea:

Baseball simulations should be open, inspectable, and forkable.

If a game matters, its history shouldn't disappear when a company loses interest.

Here's what we're building. ⚾🧵

---

Most sports games are tied to a single company, a single server, or a single release cycle.

When support ends, communities scatter and years of history can disappear.

---

We wanted to try a different approach.

Build the simulation in the open.

Make it understandable.

Make it portable.

Make it possible for others to preserve and extend.

---

At its core, EBL consists of two things:

An open baseball simulation engine.

And a shared baseball world built on top of it.

---

The simulation engine is open source.

Anyone can inspect the code, study how games are resolved, run their own league, or build something entirely different on top of it.

---

But EBL isn't just software.

It's a shared baseball world where managers compete against each other over time.

---

Every roster decision affects a franchise competing against other franchises in the same world.

---

When you join EBL, you're not creating a save file.

You're taking control of a team inside an existing baseball world.

---

Teams play games every day throughout the season.

Standings update, statistics accumulate, and league history grows as the season unfolds.

---

In EBL, you build lineups, manage your bench, organize your rotation, assign bullpen roles, and make roster decisions throughout the season.

---

Success requires more than finding a few star players.

Depth matters.

Bullpen management matters.

Long-term planning matters.

---

Games are resolved using a pitch-by-pitch simulation engine.

Every game is built one pitch at a time.

---

Each pitch can become a ball, strike, foul ball, ball in play, stolen base attempt, wild pitch, or passed ball.

The game unfolds one pitch at a time.

---

Teams play through a full 162-game season.

Standings evolve daily.

Every game becomes part of a larger league history.

---

Managers can acquire talent through free agency and the player marketplace.

Players can be bought, sold, and acquired through offers between teams.

---

Season after season, the league continues moving forward.

Standings, statistics, transactions, and championships remain part of the record.

---

EBL is designed as a long-running baseball world.

Each season builds on the ones that came before it, creating a permanent record of teams, players, and league history.

---

EBL is an online baseball world built on an open simulation engine.

Every game, player, transaction, and season becomes part of a shared history.
