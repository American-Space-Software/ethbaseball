# Your Franchise

---

When you join EBL, you take control of a baseball franchise.

From that point forward, you're responsible for managing the roster, setting lineups, organizing your pitching staff, and improving your team.

Here's what that means. ⚾🧵

---

Your franchise can have up to 40 players.

Only 26 can be active at any given time.

Managing those roster spots is one of your primary responsibilities.

---

The active roster is the group of players available for games.

Every roster decision requires a tradeoff.

Adding one player often means removing another.

---

Your active roster includes a lineup, bench, rotation, and bullpen.

Each group serves a different role throughout the season.

---

You'll build a lineup.

Decide which players start.

Set a batting order.

And determine who earns a place on the active roster.

---

You'll also manage a bench.

Bench players provide depth and give you options when your roster changes.

---

Your pitching staff is divided into two groups:

A starting rotation and a bullpen.

---

Your rotation supplies starting pitchers.

Your bullpen provides relief pitchers for the later innings.

---

Your bullpen includes closers, setup relievers, middle relievers, long relievers, and mop-up relievers.

You'll decide which pitchers fill those roles.

---

Players have ratings that reflect their abilities.

Those ratings help you evaluate strengths, weaknesses, and possible roster changes.

---

As players gain experience, they improve over time.

Managing player development is another important part of running a franchise.

---

Your team plays games throughout the season.

Every game results in either a win or a loss.

---

Those results affect your team's rating and position in the standings.

---

Successful teams earn Diamonds throughout the season.

Diamonds are the primary currency used throughout EBL.

---

Diamonds can be used to develop players and acquire talent from other franchises.

---

Every roster move has a cost.

Improving one part of your team often means giving up resources that could have been used elsewhere.

---

As a manager, you'll constantly make decisions about players, roster spots, development, and Diamonds.

---

Some decisions help immediately.

Others may take multiple seasons to pay off.

---

The goal is simple:

Build a stronger franchise and compete against the rest of the league.
