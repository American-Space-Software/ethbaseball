# Managing Your Team with Discord

---

In EBL, Discord is more than a community server.

It's one of the primary ways managers stay connected to the league.

Here's how it works. ⚾🧵

---

To get started, click "Start Playing" on the EBL website.

You'll then be asked to log in with Discord.

---

After logging in, EBL creates a new franchise and associates it with your Discord account.

That Discord account becomes your identity within the league.

---

EBL could have required managers to log in with a wallet.

We chose Discord instead.

---

Discord provides a simple way to identify managers, communicate with them, and reduce spam within the league.

---

The EBL website remains the primary place to manage your team.

Lineups.

Rotations.

Bullpen assignments.

Player transactions.

Statistics.

Games.

---

Discord complements those tools by helping managers stay connected to league activity.

---

Throughout a season, teams generate a steady stream of events.

Games are played.

Players are signed.

Marketplace activity occurs.

League standings change.

---

Discord allows EBL to deliver notifications directly to managers.

---

You can receive updates about important events involving your team and the league.

---

Notifications help you keep track of league activity even when you're away from the website.

---

Discord is also where many managers communicate with one another.

---

Managers discuss strategy.

Talk baseball.

Follow league activity.

And negotiate player transactions.

---

The #marketplace channel is a common place to advertise players, look for buyers, and find trade partners.

---

Many transactions begin with a conversation before an offer is ever submitted.

---

Communication is an important part of any online league.

Discord provides a place for managers to stay connected throughout the season.

---

The website handles baseball operations.

Discord helps managers stay informed and connected.

---

Together, they provide the day-to-day experience of managing a franchise in EBL.
