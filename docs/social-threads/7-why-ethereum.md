# Why Ethereum?

---

EBL was built around a simple idea:

Baseball worlds should be open, portable, and independent.

Many of the ideas behind EBL were influenced by the Ethereum ecosystem.

Here's what that means. ⚾🧵

---

EBL is more than a website.

It includes an open baseball simulation engine and a baseball world built on top of it.

---

The simulation engine is open source.

Anyone can inspect it.

Anyone can run it.

Anyone can build on top of it.

---

But software is only part of a baseball world.

The world itself matters too.

Teams.

Players.

Statistics.

Transactions.

League history.

---

Most online games keep all of that inside a single database controlled by a single company.

---

That works as long as the company continues operating the game.

But it makes the world difficult to preserve, study, or extend independently.

---

The first versions of the idea behind EBL started around 2020.

At the time, we were exploring what a highly decentralized baseball league might look like.

---

Early concepts included NFT players, public marketplaces, and decentralized game resolution using systems like Chainlink.

---

The goal was to build a baseball world that could exist independently of any single operator.

---

As the project evolved, we became more focused on the baseball simulation itself.

The result was a small, portable simulation engine that can run in both client and server environments.

---

That portability remains important.

It keeps open the possibility of more decentralized versions of the game in the future.

---

The version of EBL that exists today is much more practical.

It prioritizes running a baseball league over maximizing decentralization.

---

Today, EBL operates as a centrally managed league.

The goal is to provide a stable baseball world while continuing to explore ideas inspired by open systems.

---

Ethereum demonstrated a different approach.

A shared system that anyone can verify, anyone can participate in, and anyone can build on.

---

That idea influenced EBL.

Not just the technology.

The philosophy.

---

We want baseball worlds to be portable.

Not just the simulation engine.

The worlds themselves.

---

Our long-term goal is to make it possible for communities to preserve and run EBL worlds independently.

---

That means preserving more than code.

It means preserving the teams, players, statistics, transactions, and history that make a league meaningful.

---

Diamonds are part of that vision.

---

Diamonds are implemented as an ERC-20 token called EBLD.

You can view the contract here:

https://etherscan.io/address/0xc7820962eB333Ee7A9494455392f79699127B013

---

Because EBLD exists independently of the EBL database, it can be used by other applications and contracts outside the league itself.

---

That portability is important.

It allows value to exist outside any individual baseball world.

---

Today, EBL operates a single baseball world.

The systems behind it are designed to allow other communities to build their own.

---

Ethereum provides a shared foundation that exists beyond any individual server, league, or operator.

That same principle influences how we think about baseball worlds.
