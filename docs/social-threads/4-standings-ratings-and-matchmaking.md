# Standings, Ratings, and Matchmaking

---

Every team in EBL has a rating.

EBL uses the Glicko-2 rating system to estimate team strength and create competitive matchups.

Here's how ratings, matchmaking, and standings work. ⚾🧵

---

A team's rating is based on its results against other teams.

Winning against strong opponents generally helps more than winning against weaker opponents.

---

Likewise, losing to a strong opponent is different from losing to a weaker opponent.

Glicko-2 considers both the result and the strength of the teams involved.

---

The standings are ordered by rating.

Teams with higher ratings appear above teams with lower ratings.

---

Because ratings change throughout the season, the standings change throughout the season as well.

---

Before a team can play, it must enter the queue.

The queue is where teams wait to be matched against opponents.

---

When teams enter the queue, EBL uses their ratings to find opponents.

Teams with similar ratings are generally matched together.

---

This helps create games between teams of similar strength whenever possible.

---

Not every matchup will be perfectly equal.

The available teams in the queue change throughout the day.

---

When a matchup is found, the game begins immediately.

Teams can play up to one game per league day.

---

Games produce wins and losses.

Those results affect both team records and team ratings.

---

Strong results against strong opponents can produce larger rating gains.

The opposite is also true.

---

A team's record and rating often move together.

But they measure different things.

---

Your record tracks wins and losses.

Your rating estimates team strength based on those results.

---

Ratings are also used when determining daily game rewards.

The most competitive matchups receive the highest rewards.

---

A close matchup currently earns approximately 100 Diamonds for both teams.

Larger rating differences reduce those rewards.

---

Teams must complete at least 130 games during the season to qualify for season-end rewards.

---

Qualifying teams receive 50 Diamonds for every win they earned during the season.

More wins result in larger rewards.

---

Glicko-2 helps create competitive matchups, track team strength, and organize the standings throughout the season.

---

The standings update throughout the season as ratings change and games are played.
